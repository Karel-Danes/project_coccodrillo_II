Sidescroller Starter Pack
=========================

16 side-view characters × 6 clips + ground terrain + packed atlas — 512 frames

A side-view action starter set: sixteen characters (adventurer, knight, mage, rogue, villager, merchant and ten enemies from bat to golem, dark knight to wraith), each with Idle, Walk, Jump, Attack, Hurt and Death, plus a tileable ground strip with 32-px slices and top/fill tiles, and a packed atlas with JSON. All characters face right by design — flip in-engine and you are done, with no doubled atlas.

CONTENTS

- 16 characters × 6 clips = 512 animation frames + 16 static poses (+ small variants)
- 96 animation strips + per-frame PNGs + one packed atlas (atlas/sidescroller_starter.png + JSON)
- Terrain: ground strip 96×32 px + 3 32-px slices + top/fill tiles for vertical tiling

TECHNICAL

- View: side · all characters face right — flip in-engine for the other side (no left-facing frames shipped, by design)
- Frame counts exclude the pose-echo first frame; the standing pose is a separate _static.png per character
- Per-character canvas sizes in the table (no padding to a common canvas)
- ↻ = looping clip (Idle/Walk/Run…); Attack/Hurt/Death/Jump play once
- Transparent PNG sheets/strips, ready to import into any engine (Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Phaser, …).
- metadata.json lists every file, frame count and size.
